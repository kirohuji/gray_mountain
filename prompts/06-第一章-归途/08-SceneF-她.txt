【场景说明】
探索完成触发。电话响了——吉姆的声音被低频嗡鸣覆盖。
然后——卧室传来声音：布料摩擦，光脚踩在木地板上。
汤姆转身。艾琳娜站在卧室门口。
不是疗养院末期的样子——是健康的、有血色的、有光泽的。
她微笑着走过来，手指贴上他的脸颊。温的。软的。

窗外开始飘落灰白色的细尘——不是雪。只有他的窗户在落。
他口袋里的石子振动。每分钟六次。

center of his apartment living room, back to the viewer, turned
toward the corridor. In the doorway at the end of the corridor
stands Elena — his wife — healthy, warm, alive. She wears a pale
house dress, her hair soft with the luster of health, her cheeks
pink, her lips pink, her brown eyes clear. She smiles gently,
one hand raised, fingers about to touch his face. It is a moment
of impossible tenderness. Behind him, through the window, a fine
greyish-white dust falls — like snow but not snow — settling

heartbeat. The watercolor washes bleed at the edges of the scene
like fog consuming a memory. Charcoal shadows are darker than

surface, and what lies beneath the surface is not blank.
