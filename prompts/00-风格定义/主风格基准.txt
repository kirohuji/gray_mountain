    主风格基准 — 混合媒材：水彩 + 墨线 + 炭笔 (V4.5)
================================================================================

【设计理念】
《灰山》是克苏鲁式心理恐怖视觉小说。美术风格应体现：
- 水彩的渗开与不可控 → 呼应"雾"和"边界模糊"的恐怖
- 炭笔的粉末粗糙 → 呼应石灰岩山体、肺部病变的纹理
- 墨线的手工不完美 → 角色有辨识度，避免 AI 流水线感
- 手工纸纹理 → 天然做旧，所有图层共享同一基底

【UI 主题兼容】
ess Black & White Horror UI Theme 的 weathered 美学与本风格兼容：
- UI 的"风雨侵蚀" → 对应水彩的渗色和纸纹
- UI 的"纤细白线" → 对应墨线的利落勾勒
- UI 的"深黑背景" → 对应炭笔的浓重阴影

【自然语言风格描述 — 放在标签后面】
================================================================================

. A mixed media illustration combining watercolor washes, fine 
dip-pen ink lines, and charcoal shading on rough watercolor 
paper. The watercolor bleeds and granulates organically at the 
edges, creating wet atmospheric washes for backgrounds and fog. 
Charcoal powder smudges build deep shadows with a rough powdery 
texture like limestone dust. Ink lines are hand-drawn and slightly 
uneven — organic, not mechanical — defining character silhouettes 
and architectural details with deliberate imperfection. The 
overall palette is monochrome with sepia and umber undertones, 
like a vintage naturalist's field sketch that has been touched 
by something darker. The handmade quality is essential — visible 
brushstrokes, ink bleeds, paper grain. This is not digital art 
pretending to be traditional; it is a traditional media aesthetic 
rendered with the textures of water, pigment, charcoal dust, and 
rough paper.
