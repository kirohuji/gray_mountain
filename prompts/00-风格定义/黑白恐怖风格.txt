【恐怖在媒材中的表达】
- 水彩渗色 → 血在湿纸上扩散、雾气吞噬边缘
- 炭笔抹开 → 阴影像活的、粉末像骨灰
- 墨线断裂 → 现实在溶解、轮廓在消失
- 纸纹透出 → 一切都是画在纸上的——但纸下面有什么？

================================================================================
【自然语言恐怖描述】
================================================================================

. The horror is not in what is shown but in what the medium itself 
reveals — watercolor bleeding beyond its intended boundaries like 
fog consuming a landscape, charcoal shadows smudging deeper than 
they should as if something is moving inside the pigment, ink 
lines breaking where reality grows thin. The paper texture shows 
through everything — a reminder that this is a surface, and 
surfaces can be scratched away. The creeping dread of lovecraftian 
horror expressed not through monsters but through the dissolution 
of form itself. Boundaries blur. Edges bleed. The image is 
unstable — and what lies beneath the instability is not blank 
paper but something waiting.
